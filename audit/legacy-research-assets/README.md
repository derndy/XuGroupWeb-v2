# Legacy Research assets

These files are retained for audit and source recovery. They are deliberately outside Hugo's `content/` and `static/` trees so Netlify does not publish them as website assets.

Neither image may be moved into a public asset directory or referenced by a page until its record in `data/research_assets.yml` has complete provenance, rights, caption, alternative text, evidence status, publication state, owner, review date, and PI approval.
