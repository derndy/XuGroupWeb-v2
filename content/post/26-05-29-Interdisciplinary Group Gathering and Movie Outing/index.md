Interdisciplinary Gathering with the Wang and Lin Research Groups
<!--more-->

On May 29, 2026, our group joined the research group of Prof. Yinghang Wang from the School of Law at South China Normal University and the research group of Prof. Zhenghao Lin from the School of Architecture at South China University of Technology for an interdisciplinary gathering.

The event began with lunch, during which students from the three groups introduced themselves and shared their academic backgrounds and research interests. The conversations brought together perspectives from biomedical science and engineering, law, and architecture, creating opportunities for meaningful exchange across different disciplines.

After lunch, the groups watched the film *Dear You* together. The gathering was made possible by the longstanding friendship among Prof. Xu, Prof. Wang, and Prof. Lin, and was intended to encourage students to broaden their perspectives, learn about research beyond their own fields, and build connections across disciplines.
