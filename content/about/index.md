We design how scientific systems represent information, organize interactions, learn from evidence, and generate new knowledge and designs.

Our research connects learning-system design, evidence engineering, and mathematical exploration. Molecular and material systems provide demanding settings in which these ideas can be developed and tested.

Prediction is a starting point. We ask what structure, interaction, or mechanism could explain a pattern, and what evidence would distinguish that explanation from alternatives.
